Welcome

Not just what to play.
Where to bring it in.

Two records can share a tempo and a key and still wreck the mix — the kicks land on different sixteenths, or both singers arrive at once. Cuepoint listens to the parts that actually decide it.

1
Pick the record that's playing. It's the list on the left — search it, or filter by BPM, key and genre.
2
Read the score. Every track in your crate is rated against it on seven things, not two. Open a card to see which one is holding it back.
3
Take the mix point. Each result tells you where to leave the outgoing record, where to come in on the new one, and how many bars to run them together.
4
Hear it before you play it. Press play on any result. Drag the lavender line to move where the bass changes hands, and use the EQ kills if something clashes.
